## Saws

### Crosscut Saw

Cuts across the grain. Teeth are angled to sever wood fibers.

### Rip Saw

Cuts along the grain. Teeth are chisel-shaped to remove material.

### Japanese Pull Saw

Cuts on the pull stroke. Thinner blade allows finer cuts.

### Coping Saw

Thin blade in a frame for curved cuts and interior cutouts.

## Chisels

-   **Bench chisels** - General purpose, various widths
-   **Mortise chisels** - Heavy duty for chopping mortises
-   **Paring chisels** - Long, thin for fine work

**Tip:** Always chisel away from your body. Keep chisels sharp.

## Planes

-   **Block plane** - Small, one-handed, for end grain and chamfers
-   **Smoothing plane** - Final surface preparation
-   **Jack plane** - General purpose flattening

## Other Hand Tools

-   **Mallet** - For striking chisels
-   **Rasp/File** - Shaping and smoothing
-   **Scraper** - Removing thin shavings, smoothing
