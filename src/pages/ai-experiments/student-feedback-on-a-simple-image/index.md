Testing ChatGPT's ability to give constructive feedback on student creative work.

## Context

In creative coding classes, students often create simple visual outputs. I tested whether ChatGPT could provide useful, encouraging feedback on beginner work.

## Prompting Strategies

### Encouraging Feedback

"Give feedback as an encouraging instructor. Start with what works well."

### Technical Feedback

"What could be improved from a technical perspective?"

### Next Steps

"Suggest three ways the student could extend this project."

## Results

-   Good at identifying what's working
-   Suggestions were appropriate for skill level
-   Needed guidance on what level of feedback to give

## Tips for Educators

-   Specify the student's experience level
-   Ask for specific types of feedback separately
-   Review before sharing with students
