Create an inspiration board to help develop your final project concept.

## What is an Inspiration Board?

An inspiration board (or mood board) is a collection of images, materials, and references that capture the feeling and direction of your project. It helps communicate your vision and serves as a reference during construction.

## What to Include

-   Images of similar objects or styles you admire
-   Color palettes and material samples
-   Sketches of your ideas
-   Reference images for joinery or techniques
-   Context images showing intended use or environment

## How to Create One

### Digital

-   Pinterest board
-   Figma or Miro board
-   Simple image collage

### Physical

-   Poster board with printed images
-   Wood samples and material swatches
-   Sketches and notes
