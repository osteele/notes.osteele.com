Create a decorative box with drawers using the band saw.

## What is a Band Saw Box?

A band saw box is made from a single block of wood. The exterior shape and interior drawers are cut out using a band saw, then glued back together. This technique allows for organic, curved shapes that would be difficult to achieve with traditional joinery.

## Objectives

-   Master band saw curves and resawing
-   Plan cuts to create functional interior spaces
-   Practice gluing curved surfaces
-   Create a finished, functional object

## Process

1.  Select a thick block of wood (at least 3" thick)
2.  Draw your design on the wood
3.  Cut off the back piece
4.  Cut out drawer shapes
5.  Hollow out drawers
6.  Glue back piece on
7.  Sand and finish

## Tips

-   Use a fine blade for smoother cuts
-   Plan your cut sequence carefully
-   Leave enough material for drawer fronts
-   Sand inside surfaces before final assembly
