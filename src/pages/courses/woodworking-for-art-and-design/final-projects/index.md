The final project is your opportunity to design and build something of your own choosing.

## Requirements

-   Must incorporate techniques learned in class
-   Must include at least one type of joinery
-   Must be finished appropriately for intended use
-   Must be documented according to guidelines

## Timeline

1.  **Week 1:** Proposal and sketches
2.  **Week 2:** Materials list and begin construction
3.  **Week 3:** Construction continues
4.  **Week 4:** Finishing and documentation
5.  **Week 5:** Presentations

## Grading Criteria

-   Design (creativity, functionality): 25%
-   Craftsmanship (quality of execution): 35%
-   Process (planning, iteration): 20%
-   Documentation and presentation: 20%
