An experimental short fiction piece written with ChatGPT exploring themes of recursion and self-reference.

## Concept

Inspired by Douglas Hofstadter's work on strange loops and self-referential structures, I asked ChatGPT to write a short story that references its own creation.

## The Experiment

Prompts explored:

-   Write a story about an AI writing a story about an AI
-   Include a character who realizes they're in a narrative
-   Have the story question its own existence

## Themes

-   Self-reference and recursion
-   The nature of creativity
-   Boundaries between author and creation
-   Meta-fiction traditions

## Observations

ChatGPT handles meta-narrative competently, drawing on its training data of self-referential literature. The results range from clever to slightly unsettling.
