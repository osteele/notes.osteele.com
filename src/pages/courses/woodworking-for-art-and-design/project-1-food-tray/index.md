Create a simple food tray to practice fundamental woodworking skills.

## Objectives

-   Practice accurate measuring and marking
-   Make straight cuts with hand or power saws
-   Assemble pieces with glue and/or fasteners
-   Apply a food-safe finish

## Specifications

-   Approximate size: 12" x 8" x 1"
-   Must have raised edges to contain items
-   Finish must be food-safe

## Process

1.  Sketch your design with dimensions
2.  Select and purchase wood
3.  Cut pieces to size
4.  Sand all pieces before assembly
5.  Glue and clamp
6.  Final sanding and finishing

## Tips

-   Choose a wood that's easy to work with (pine, poplar)
-   Mineral oil is an easy food-safe finish
-   Consider adding handles for easier carrying
