Different types of wood glue and their appropriate uses.

## PVA (White/Yellow) Glue

**Examples:** Titebond, Elmer's Wood Glue

-   Most common wood glue
-   Strong bond, dries clear to yellow
-   Not waterproof (Titebond III is water-resistant)
-   Open time: 5-10 minutes
-   Clamp time: 30-60 minutes
-   Full cure: 24 hours

## Polyurethane Glue

**Examples:** Gorilla Glue

-   Waterproof
-   Expands as it cures (can fill gaps)
-   Works on more materials than PVA
-   Requires moisture to cure
-   Foams up - use sparingly

## Epoxy

-   Two-part adhesive (resin + hardener)
-   Fills gaps well
-   Waterproof
-   Can be colored or made clear
-   Various cure times available

## CA (Super) Glue

-   Very fast bond
-   Good for small repairs
-   Brittle - not for structural joints
-   Can be used with accelerator

## Tips

-   Apply thin, even layer to both surfaces
-   Glue squeeze-out indicates good coverage
-   Wipe excess with damp cloth before it dries
-   Proper clamping is essential
