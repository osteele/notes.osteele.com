## Schedule

The course meets weekly in the woodshop. Each session includes demonstration, practice time, and project work.

## Materials

### Provided

-   Shop tools and equipment
-   Safety gear (goggles, ear protection)
-   Sandpaper and finishing supplies

### Student Purchases

-   Wood for projects (see [wood buying assignment](/courses/woodworking-for-art-and-design/assignment-buy-a-piece-of-wood/))
-   Personal safety glasses (recommended)

## Shop Safety

All students must complete shop safety training before using power tools. Safety rules include:

-   Always wear safety glasses in the shop
-   No loose clothing or jewelry near machinery
-   Tie back long hair
-   Use hearing protection with loud tools
-   Ask before using unfamiliar equipment
