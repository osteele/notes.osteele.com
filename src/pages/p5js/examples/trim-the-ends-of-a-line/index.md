Link: https://www.openprocessing.org/sketch/1045123

I needed to [draw an arrow](https://www.openprocessing.org/sketch/1045212). I also needed to [adjust its end points](https://www.openprocessing.org/sketch/1045123), so that they did not overlap with the labels that the arrows joined. This function moves a line's endpoints so that the line has the same slope, but beings and ends *outside* of a couple of rectangles that specify where to save room for a label.
