## Essential Starter Tools

### For Measuring

-   Tape measure
-   Combination square
-   Pencil

### For Cutting

-   Hand saw or pull saw
-   Utility knife

### For Assembly

-   Hammer or mallet
-   Screwdriver set
-   Clamps (at least 4)

### For Finishing

-   Sandpaper (80, 120, 220 grit)
-   Sanding block

## Tool Safety

-   Always wear safety glasses
-   Keep tools sharp - dull tools are dangerous
-   Secure workpiece before cutting
-   Know where your hands are at all times
