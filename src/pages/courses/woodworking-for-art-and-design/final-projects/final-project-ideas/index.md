Some ideas to inspire your final project. Feel free to adapt these or come up with something entirely your own.

## Functional Objects

-   **Desk organizer** - Compartments for pens, cards, phone
-   **Cutting board** - End-grain or edge-grain design
-   **Bookends** - Weighted or friction-based
-   **Picture frame** - With interesting joinery
-   **Lamp base** - Combined with electrical components
-   **Clock** - Wall or desk style

## Furniture

-   **Small stool** - Three or four legs
-   **Side table** - Minimalist design
-   **Shelf** - Floating or with brackets
-   **Magazine rack** - Floor or wall mounted

## Decorative

-   **Sculpture** - Abstract or representational
-   **Wall art** - Geometric patterns or relief carving
-   **Wooden toys** - Vehicles, puzzles, games

## Considerations

-   Match complexity to your skill level
-   Consider available time and materials
-   Think about who will use it and how
