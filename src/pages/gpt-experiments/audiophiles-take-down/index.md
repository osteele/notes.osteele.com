Asking ChatGPT to write humorous critiques in the style of an overly particular audiophile.

## The Prompt

"Write a scathing audiophile review of \[everyday sound\]. Be pedantic about frequency response, soundstage, and imaging."

## Examples

### Review of a Microwave Beep

ChatGPT generated an absurdly detailed critique of the microwave beep's "lack of warmth in the mid-range" and "compressed dynamic range."

### Review of a Car Horn

Complaints about "muddy bass response" and "harsh treble peaks."

## Why It Works

-   ChatGPT knows audiophile vocabulary well
-   The juxtaposition of technical language with mundane sounds is inherently funny
-   Good example of creative prompting
