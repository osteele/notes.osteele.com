Using CNC machines to create precise slots for joinery and assembly.

## What are CNC Slots?

CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machines can cut precise slots that allow pieces to interlock without traditional joinery. This technique is popular for flat-pack furniture and laser-cut designs.

## Design Considerations

### Slot Width

-   Should match material thickness
-   Account for kerf (cutting width)
-   Test fit with scrap material first

### Slot Depth

-   Typically 50-75% of material thickness
-   Deeper slots = stronger joint but weaker material

### Tolerances

-   Tight fit: 0.005" smaller than material
-   Sliding fit: exact size or 0.005" larger
-   Loose fit: 0.010-0.015" larger

## Common Patterns

-   **Tab and slot** - Basic interlocking
-   **Finger joints** - Box corners
-   **T-slots** - For adjustable connections

## Software

Design slots in:

-   Fusion 360
-   VCarve
-   Inkscape (for 2D designs)
