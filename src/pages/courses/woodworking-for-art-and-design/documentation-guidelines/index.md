Good documentation is essential for creative work. It helps you reflect on your process and share your work with others.

## What to Document

-   **Process photos** - Work in progress at each major stage
-   **Final photos** - Multiple angles, good lighting
-   **Sketches and plans** - Initial designs and iterations
-   **Written reflection** - What you learned, challenges faced

## Photography Tips

-   Use natural light when possible
-   Choose a clean, neutral background
-   Include something for scale
-   Capture details and joinery
-   Show the object in use if applicable

## Written Documentation

For each project, include:

1.  Brief description of the piece
2.  Materials and dimensions
3.  Techniques used
4.  Challenges and solutions
5.  What you would do differently

## Submission Format

Submit documentation as a PDF or web page with embedded images.
